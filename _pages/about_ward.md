## **Ward** Gauderis

Hello! I'm an FWO PhD Fellow studying **compositionality** as a mathematical foundation for deep learning. I use tensor networks and category theory to analyse neural representations as induced by the computations encoded in a model's weight structure.

Instead of reading tea leaves in activation space, I treat the model's weights as a formal compositional system, where local mechanisms compose into global properties. Emergent behaviour then becomes a direct function of the algebraic wiring, and the black box divides into parts small enough to conquer.

My work proposes **tensor models** to bridge neuro-symbolic AI and mechanistic interpretability, asking how models compose concepts to generalise... or not.
