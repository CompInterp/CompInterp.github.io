## **Ward** Gauderis

Hello! I'm an FWO PhD Fellow in Brussels studying **compositionality** as a mathematical foundation for deep learning. I use tensor networks and category theory to ground the analysis of neural representations in the weight geometry of their architecture.

**How can _compositional design_ improve _compositional behaviour_?**

My work proposes **tensor models** as a unified framework for tractable neuro-symbolic integration and mechanistic analysis. By combining structural constraints with MDL  to disentangle spurious interactions, I aim to enable truly compositional generalisation and interpretability. Learn more [here](https://wardgauderis.github.io/)!
